# Executive Summary: The Consulting Plan

*A high-level overview of the consulting business model, designed to explain the strategy, risk mitigation, and financial upside in plain English.*

## 1. The Goal
The objective is to launch a solo technology consulting business targeting local companies in the Kalamazoo/Portage area. The financial goal is to generate $1,000 to $2,000 a month in extra income to start, by taking on 1 or 2 small, highly scoped projects per month, with the potential to grow into monthly recurring revenue.

## 2. What I Will Actually Be Doing (The Product)
I am not building the next Facebook or taking on massive, multi-year software projects. I am acting as a "digital plumber." Local businesses (like gyms, accounting firms, and marketing agencies) waste hundreds of hours a week manually copying and pasting data between software that doesn't talk to each other. 

I write simple, automated scripts that do this boring work for them. For example, writing a script that automatically pulls invoices from an email and puts them into a database. It takes me a weekend to build, and it saves them 20 hours a week. They gladly pay $1,000+ for that time savings.

## 3. How We Are Protecting Our Family (Risk Mitigation)
The biggest concern with starting a business is legal and financial risk. We have designed a strict system to ensure our personal assets (house, savings) are 100% shielded from the business.
*   **The Corporate Shield:** I will file a Michigan LLC for $50. This legally separates my business from our personal finances. [See the LLC Guide](../how-to/form_michigan_llc.md).
*   **The Financial Shield:** I will not write a single line of code for a client until I purchase Business Insurance (Errors & Omissions), which costs about $60/month. If a client sues, the insurance pays for it, not us. [See the Insurance Breakdown](../reference/business_insurance.md).
*   **The Paper Shield:** I am purchasing a legally vetted Master Services Agreement (MSA) that forces clients to agree that my total liability can never exceed what they paid me. [See the MSA Strategy](../reference/master_services_agreement.md).

## 4. How I Will Find Clients (The Pipeline)
I will not be making sleazy cold calls or spending money on Facebook ads. The strategy is highly targeted and local.
1.  I look at local job boards to see who is hiring for "Data Entry."
2.  I reach out and offer a free 15-minute technical audit.
3.  I ask them what their most annoying, manual Friday task is.
4.  I offer to automate it for a flat fee.
*   **Deep Dive:** [View the Master Business Pipelines](../explanation/master_business_pipelines.md)

## 5. Why This Will Work
I already possess the skills to do this. I have been building incredibly complex software architectures (like Medallion data pipelines and AI orchestration systems) for free in my own time. The work I will be doing for these local clients is significantly easier than my hobby projects, but it carries immense financial value for them. By strictly controlling the scope of the work and heavily protecting myself legally, this is a highly asymmetrical bet: very low risk for a very high reward.

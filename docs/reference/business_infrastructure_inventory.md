# Business Infrastructure & Inventory Checklist

This document serves as the chronological "shopping list" for operating a solo technical consulting business. It is sorted sequentially so that all foundational, zero-cost tasks are completed before any financial investment is made.

## Phase 1: Free & Foundational ($0)
Complete all of these steps first. They require zero financial investment.

- [ ] **Employer Identification Number (EIN)**
  *   *Cost:* $0
  *   *Details:* Acquired directly via the IRS website.
- [ ] **GitHub Organization**
  *   *Cost:* $0
  *   *Details:* GitHub Free tier includes unlimited private repositories for organizations.
- [ ] **Testing Environments**
  *   *Cost:* $0
  *   *Details:* Use open-source tools like Docker, Vagrant, or VirtualBox for local environment replication.
- [ ] **Time Tracking**
  *   *Cost:* $0
  *   *Details:* Toggl Track and Clockify both offer robust free tiers for solo users.
- [ ] **Meeting Software**
  *   *Cost:* $0
  *   *Details:* Google Meet (requires Google account) or Zoom Basic (40-minute limit).
- [ ] **Business Checking Account**
  *   *Cost:* $0
  *   *Details:* Banks like Novo, Bluevine, or Relay offer business checking with zero monthly fees and zero minimum balances.
- [ ] **Invoicing & Accounting**
  *   *Cost:* $0 monthly
  *   *Details:* Wave Accounting is 100% free for bookkeeping and generating invoices. You only pay standard processing fees (like Stripe) when a client actually pays you via credit card.

## Phase 2: Low-Cost & Essential Upgrades ($)
Invest in these once Phase 1 is complete and you are preparing to market to clients.

- [ ] **Michigan LLC Filing**
  *   *Cost:* $50 (One-time state fee)
  *   *Free Alternative:* None. This is a mandatory state fee to gain corporate liability shielding.
- [ ] **Domain Name & Professional Email**
  *   *Cost:* ~$15/year (Domain) + $6/month (Google Workspace)
  *   *Free Alternative:* You must buy the domain name (~$15), but **Zoho Mail** offers a "Forever Free" plan for up to 5 users allowing you to connect your custom domain for professional email without the monthly fee.
- [ ] **Website Hosting**
  *   *Cost:* $5 - $20/month
  *   *Free Alternative:* **GitHub Pages** or **Netlify** offer generous free tiers for hosting static websites, Vue/React Single Page Apps, and portfolios.
- [ ] **Digital Signatures (MSA/SOW Execution)**
  *   *Cost:* $15/month (DocuSign/HelloSign)
  *   *Free Alternative:* **Documenso** is a highly professional open-source alternative to DocuSign that you can self-host for free. Alternatively, Dropbox Sign (formerly HelloSign) allows up to 3 free legally binding document signatures per month.

## Phase 3: Legal & High-Tier Expenses ($$$)
Invest in these right before you sign your first paying client. Do not spend this money until you have verbal agreement on a project.

- [ ] **Business Insurance (E&O + General Liability)**
  *   *Cost:* $60 - $120/month
  *   *Free Alternative:* None. Do not operate without this. Wait to purchase it until a client agrees to an SOW, but factor the cost into your pricing.
- [ ] **Legal Counsel (Master Services Agreement)**
  *   *Cost:* $500 - $800 (Flat-fee via UpCounsel)
  *   *Cheaper Alternative:* Use **Common Paper** (an open-source standard contract project for SaaS and consulting) or purchase a vetted template from LegalZoom/Bonsai for ~$50. However, paying a lawyer once ensures the liability limits protect you under specific Michigan laws.

# Business Insurance Reference

While an LLC protects your personal assets, business insurance protects your business's capital from being drained by legal defense fees or settlements. 

## 1. Technology Errors and Omissions (E&O) / Professional Liability
This is the critical insurance for a technical consultant. It covers financial damages a client suffers due to your mistakes, bad advice, or failure to deliver.

*   **What it covers:** 
    *   Code updates that cause business interruption (e.g., taking an e-commerce site offline).
    *   Data loss due to misconfigurations (e.g., deleting a database or exposing an AWS bucket).
    *   Breach of contract for missing critical software delivery deadlines.
*   **Target Coverage Limit:** $1,000,000 per occurrence / $1,000,000 aggregate. This is the industry standard required by most corporate vendor agreements.
*   **Estimated Cost:** $40 to $90 per month.
*   *Note on Cyber Liability:* Ensure the Tech E&O policy includes a "Cyber Liability" rider if you will be handling databases, as this specifically covers data breaches.

## 2. General Liability Insurance
This covers basic physical risks and property damage. Even for remote workers, many corporate clients legally require this policy to sign a contract.

*   **What it covers:**
    *   Physical damage to client property (e.g., damaging a server rack during an on-site visit).
    *   Third-party bodily injury at your workspace.
    *   Slander, libel, or copyright infringement claims in marketing.
*   **Target Coverage Limit:** $1,000,000 per occurrence.
*   **Estimated Cost:** $20 to $40 per month.

# Symptom Tests for Product Alignment

Clients rarely know the technical terminology for the solutions they need (e.g., they will not ask for "Medallion Architecture" or "CI/CD Pipelines"). As a consultant, you must diagnose their operational symptoms to align them with the correct technical product bucket. 

Use the following diagnostic tests during discovery to bucket the client correctly.

## 1. The "Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V" Test
**Target Bucket:** Backend Architecture & Data Engineering (ETL Pipelines, API Integrations)

**The Symptom:** 
The business is bleeding human capital on manual data transfer. Employees spend significant portions of their week exporting CSV files from one system, manipulating them in Excel, and manually uploading or typing that data into a second system (e.g., moving leads from a CRM into an accounting tool, or updating inventory across two storefronts).

**Diagnostic Questions to Ask:**
*   "How much time does your staff spend copying and pasting data between your different software tools?"
*   "If someone forgets to run the Friday spreadsheet report, does your executive team lose visibility into the business?"

**The Solution Pitch:**
"I build automated pipelines that connect your software securely in the background, eliminating manual data entry and ensuring your dashboards are updated in real-time without human intervention."

---

## 2. The "Server Fear" Test
**Target Bucket:** DevOps & Infrastructure Automation (CI/CD, IaC, Server Maintenance)

**The Symptom:** 
The business relies on a digital tool, website, or internal application, but deployments are manual, brittle, and feared. They experience random downtime, updates require taking the system offline for hours, and testing is done by "uploading files to the live server and hoping it works."

**Diagnostic Questions to Ask:**
*   "When you need to update your internal app or website, are you worried it might crash the system?"
*   "If your current server hardware failed right now, how long would it take to get your business back online?"

**The Solution Pitch:**
"I implement modern deployment pipelines that automatically test your code and deploy it with zero downtime. We will turn your manual server setup into code, so if a disaster happens, we can spin up a perfect clone of your business in minutes."

---

## 3. The "Reading Fatigue" Test
**Target Bucket:** Advanced Automation & AI Integration (Semantic Parsing, Agentic Workflows)

**The Symptom:** 
Highly paid professionals (lawyers, accountants, compliance officers, HR managers) are acting as human routers. They spend hours reading unstructured text (PDFs, long email threads, support tickets) just to categorize them, extract a few key data points, or decide who should handle them.

**Diagnostic Questions to Ask:**
*   "Are your highest-paid employees spending hours just reading documents to find specific clauses or data points?"
*   "How many emails or tickets come into your support inbox that are just manually forwarded to someone else?"

**The Solution Pitch:**
"I build private, secure AI workflows that can read, categorize, and extract data from thousands of documents in seconds, allowing your team to focus on high-value decision-making instead of manual reading."

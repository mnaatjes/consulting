# Master Services Agreement Reference

The Master Services Agreement (MSA) is the foundational legal contract between your consulting LLC and a client. It establishes the global rules for your working relationship, allowing you to bypass heavy legal negotiation for individual projects.

## The Two-Document System
Modern consulting relies on separating legal rules from project specifics:

1.  **The MSA:** Signed once per client. Contains no pricing, timelines, or specific technical deliverables. It dictates IP ownership, liability, and payment terms.
2.  **The Statement of Work (SOW):** Signed for every new project. A 1-2 page document detailing the exact technical deliverables (the "Definition of Done") and the flat-fee price. It includes a clause stating: *"This SOW is governed by the terms of the Master Services Agreement signed on [Date]."*

## Core Defensive Clauses
Your MSA serves as a "Paper Shield" and must include these non-negotiable clauses:

*   **Limitation of Liability (The Cap):** Explicitly states that your maximum financial liability for any lawsuit, failure, or bug is capped at the total amount the client paid you for the specific SOW in question.
*   **Independent Contractor Status:** Affirms you are a B2B vendor, protecting the client from IRS employee misclassification and payroll tax liabilities.
*   **Intellectual Property (IP) Transfer Trigger:** States that you retain 100% copyright ownership of the codebase and architecture *until the final invoice is paid in full*. This provides leverage against non-payment.
*   **Warranty Disclaimer ("As-Is"):** Legally defines that after a specific acceptance window (e.g., 14 days post-delivery), the software is provided "As-Is." You are not liable for fixing future bugs or maintaining servers for free unless a maintenance SOW is signed.

# Auxiliary Legal Documents Reference

Outside of the Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Statement of Work (SOW), a consulting business requires a suite of operational and public-facing legal documents to maintain compliance and protect proprietary information.

## 1. Client Acquisition Documents

### Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA)
*   **Purpose:** Ensures neither party steals trade secrets or publicly discloses sensitive data.
*   **When to Use:** Required before the technical discovery phase if the client is going to expose private codebases, server architecture, or internal financial data to you. 
*   **Key Consideration:** Always enforce a *Mutual* NDA rather than a one-way NDA to protect your own proprietary consulting frameworks and audit methodologies.

### Form W-9 (Request for Taxpayer Identification Number)
*   **Purpose:** A standard IRS form used to confirm your LLC's tax classification and Employer Identification Number (EIN).
*   **When to Use:** The client's accounting department will legally require this document before they issue your first payment.
*   **Best Practice:** Keep a pre-signed, PDF copy of your W-9 securely stored and ready to send with your first invoice.

## 2. Public-Facing Documents (Website & Marketing)

If your consulting business maintains a public web presence, you are subject to data privacy laws and intellectual property protections.

### Privacy Policy
*   **Purpose:** A legal statement explaining how you collect, use, store, and protect user data.
*   **Legal Requirement:** Required by strict data privacy laws (e.g., CCPA in California, GDPR in Europe) if your website collects *any* data. This includes passive data collection (Google Analytics) or active collection (Contact forms requesting names and emails).

### Terms of Service / Terms of Use
*   **Purpose:** Establishes the legal rules for visitors interacting with your website.
*   **Legal Requirement:** Protects your intellectual property (preventing competitors from scraping your portfolio text) and legally disclaims that any technical articles, tutorials, or blog posts on your site are "for informational purposes only" and do not constitute formal consulting advice.

### Testimonial / Marketing Release Form
*   **Purpose:** A one-page agreement granting you legal permission to use a client's logo, company name, and project metrics in your marketing materials.
*   **Legal Requirement:** Standard MSAs contain strict confidentiality clauses. You cannot legally mention a client on your portfolio or use their project as a case study without this explicit written consent.

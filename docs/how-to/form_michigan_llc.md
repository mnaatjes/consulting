# How to Form a Michigan LLC for Consulting

This guide outlines the exact, step-by-step process for legally registering your consulting business as a Single-Member LLC in the State of Michigan, establishing your corporate shield against liability.

## Step 1: Choose and Verify Your Business Name
Your business name must be distinguishable from other businesses registered in Michigan and must include "Limited Liability Company" or an abbreviation like "LLC" or "L.L.C."

1.  **Search for availability:** Use the [Michigan LARA Business Entity Search](https://cofs.lara.state.mi.us/SearchApi/Search/Search) to ensure your desired name is not already taken.

## Step 2: Determine Your Registered Agent
Michigan law requires every LLC to have a Registered Agent with a physical street address (no P.O. Boxes) in Michigan. This person or entity receives official legal and tax correspondence on behalf of the business.

*   **Option A (Free): Be your own Registered Agent.** You can use your home address. *Warning:* Your home address will become public record on the state website.
*   **Option B (Indirect Cost: $50–$150/year): Hire a Commercial Registered Agent.** If you want privacy, you can pay a company (like Northwest Registered Agent or Michigan Registered Agent) to use their address and forward your mail.

## Step 3: File the Articles of Organization
This is the official document that brings your LLC into existence.

1.  **Navigate to the portal:** Go to the [Michigan LARA Corporations Online Filing System (COFS)](https://cofs.lara.state.mi.us/corpweb/LoginSystem/US_LoginSystem.aspx).
2.  **Select filing type:** Choose "700 - Articles of Organization".
3.  **Fill out the form:** You will need to provide your business name, the purpose of the business (e.g., "Software Consulting Services"), and your Registered Agent details.
4.  **Pay the fee:** The standard filing fee is **$50.00**. 
5.  **Processing:** Standard processing usually takes a few business days. You will receive a filed copy of your Articles of Organization via email.

## Step 4: Obtain an Employer Identification Number (EIN)
An EIN is like a Social Security Number for your business. You need this to open a business bank account and file taxes.

1.  **Wait for approval:** Do not apply for an EIN until LARA has approved your Articles of Organization and your LLC officially exists.
2.  **Apply for free:** Go to the [IRS Online EIN Assistant](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online).
3.  **Process:** The application takes about 5 minutes, is completely **free**, and you will receive your EIN immediately upon completion. *Do not pay a third-party service to do this for you.*

## Step 5: Open a Business Bank Account
To maintain your liability protection (the "corporate shield"), you must never mix personal and business funds.

1.  Take your approved Articles of Organization and your new EIN letter to a bank.
2.  Open a dedicated Business Checking Account.
3.  Ensure all client payments go directly into this account, and all business expenses (software, servers, insurance) are paid from it.

## Ongoing Maintenance
*   **Annual Statement:** Every year by February 15th, you must file an Annual Statement with LARA and pay a **$25 fee** to keep the LLC in good standing. You will receive a reminder from the state via mail or email.

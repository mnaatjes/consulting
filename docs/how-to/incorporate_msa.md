# How to Acquire and Incorporate an MSA

This guide outlines how to legally acquire a Master Services Agreement (MSA) without writing it yourself, and how to execute it with new consulting clients.

## Step 1: Acquire a Legitimate Tech Consulting MSA
Do not attempt to write an MSA from scratch or piece together clauses from Google. You must use a legally vetted document specifically tailored for software/IT consulting.

**Option A: Purchase a Vetted Template (Most Affordable)**
Buy a standardized "Software Development MSA" or "IT Consulting MSA."
*   [LegalZoom](https://www.legalzoom.com/) (General business contracts)
*   [RocketLawyer](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/) (Subscription-based template access)
*   [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com/) (Highly recommended for freelancers/solo consultants; offers integrated MSA and SOW templates).

**Option B: Hire a Flat-Fee Tech Lawyer (Safest)**
Hire a lawyer to draft a bulletproof contract customized to your specific LLC and state laws.
*   [UpCounsel](https://www.upcounsel.com/): Post a job for "Software Consulting MSA draft" and receive flat-fee bids (usually $500–$800) from vetted corporate lawyers.

## Step 2: Prepare Your Boilerplate Documents
Once you acquire the MSA, you need to prepare your digital signing flow.

1.  Upload your MSA to a digital signing platform (e.g., DocuSign, HelloSign/Dropbox Sign).
2.  Create a blank **Statement of Work (SOW)** template. Ensure the header includes the text: *"This Statement of Work is governed by the Master Services Agreement between [Your LLC] and [Client Company], dated [Date]."*

## Step 3: Execution Workflow with a New Client
When a prospect agrees to hire you based on your proposal, follow this exact sequence:

1.  **Draft the SOW:** Fill out your SOW template with the exact technical deliverables, the definition of done, and the pricing.
2.  **Send the MSA:** Email the client the MSA for signature via your digital platform. Tell them: *"Attached is our standard Master Services Agreement covering the legal terms of our relationship."*
3.  **Send the SOW:** Once the MSA is signed, send the SOW for signature.
4.  **Commence Work:** Do not write a single line of code or provision any infrastructure until both the MSA and the SOW are signed by the client's decision-maker.

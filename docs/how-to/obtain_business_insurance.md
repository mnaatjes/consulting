# How to Obtain Business Insurance

This guide outlines the chronological steps to secure the necessary Errors & Omissions (E&O) and General Liability insurance for your consulting LLC.

## Step 1: Complete Legal Prerequisite Filings
Do not apply for business insurance under your personal name. The policy must protect the corporate entity.

1.  File your Articles of Organization and wait for state approval. (See [How to Form a Michigan LLC](form_michigan_llc.md)).
2.  Obtain your Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS.

## Step 2: Prepare Your Intake Information
Insurance carriers price risk based on the data you handle and your expected volume. Have these details ready:
*   **Estimated Annual Revenue:** It is standard to estimate a conservative number for your first year (e.g., $20,000 - $50,000). You can adjust this later.
*   **Data Types Handled:** Be prepared to answer explicitly if you will process HIPAA (health), PCI (credit card), or PII (Personally Identifiable Information). *Lying to get a cheaper rate will void your coverage if you are sued.*
*   **Services Provided:** Categorize your work accurately as "Software Consulting," "IT Systems Integration," or "Data Engineering."

## Step 3: Use a Digital Broker to Quote
Instead of calling a local agent, use specialized digital brokers to get immediate quotes.

1.  Navigate to a provider:
    *   [Hiscox Small Business](https://www.hiscox.com/)
    *   [Next Insurance](https://www.nextinsurance.com/)
    *   [TechInsurance.com](https://www.techinsurance.com/)
2.  Start the application for a **Business Owner's Policy (BOP)**, which bundles General Liability and Property insurance at a discount.
3.  Add **Technology Errors and Omissions (E&O)** as an absolute requirement.
4.  Set coverage limits to **$1,000,000 per occurrence / $1,000,000 aggregate**.

## Step 4: Finalize and Secure the Certificate of Insurance (COI)
1.  Review the quote to ensure Cyber Liability/Data Breach coverage is included within the Tech E&O.
2.  Bind the policy by paying the first monthly premium (usually $60-$120 total).
3.  Download your **Certificate of Insurance (COI)**. You will need to email a PDF copy of this document to mid-sized or enterprise clients when signing their vendor agreements to prove you meet their legal requirements.

# Master Launch Schedule

This document provides a chronological timeline to take your consulting business from zero to fully operational in 14 days, minimizing upfront financial risk by utilizing a "Just-In-Time" expenditure strategy.

## Phase 1: The Digital Baseline (Days 1 - 2)
*Focus: Establishing your public and operational digital footprint.*
*   **Total Expected Cost:** ~$15
*   **Tasks:**
    *   [ ] Purchase a custom domain name (~$15/yr).
    *   [ ] Set up professional email (via Zoho Mail for free, or Google Workspace for $6/mo).
    *   [ ] Register a GitHub Organization for the business (Free).
    *   [ ] Deploy a 1-3 page static portfolio website using GitHub Pages or Netlify (Free). Include the JSON capability audits as case studies.
    *   [ ] Set up accounts on Toggl (Time Tracking) and Wave Accounting (Free).

## Phase 2: Legal Initiation (Day 3)
*Focus: Triggering the state bureaucratic process.*
*   **Total Expected Cost:** $50
*   **Tasks:**
    *   [ ] Run a name availability check on the Michigan LARA COFS portal.
    *   [ ] File the "Articles of Organization" online as a Single-Member LLC.
    *   [ ] Pay the $50 state filing fee.

## Phase 3: Target Acquisition (Days 4 - 10)
*Focus: Utilizing the state processing wait time (usually 7-10 days) to build your sales pipeline.*
*   **Total Expected Cost:** $0
*   **Tasks:**
    *   [ ] Execute "Digital Reconnaissance" (Pipeline 2.1).
    *   [ ] Identify 20-30 local target businesses in the Kalamazoo/Portage area.
    *   [ ] Review their job boards for "Data Entry" roles and inspect their tech stacks.
    *   [ ] Draft the personalized "Trojan Horse" audit emails for each target. Do not send them yet.

## Phase 4: Corporate Activation (Day 11)
*Focus: Finalizing the legal and financial shield upon LARA approval.*
*   **Total Expected Cost:** $0
*   **Tasks:**
    *   [ ] Receive your approved Articles of Organization from the State of Michigan.
    *   [ ] Apply for an Employer Identification Number (EIN) on the IRS website (Takes 5 minutes, Free).
    *   [ ] Take the approved LLC document and EIN letter to a local bank (or online bank like Novo/Relay).
    *   [ ] Open a $0 minimum Business Checking Account to establish financial separation.

## Phase 5: Operations & "Just-In-Time" Expenses (Day 12+)
*Focus: Activating the sales pipeline and only spending money when revenue is imminent.*
*   **Total Expected Cost:** ~$100 - $800 (Only spent when closing a deal)
*   **Tasks:**
    *   [ ] Send the Trojan Horse audit emails to your 20 targets.
    *   [ ] Conduct 15-minute Discovery Calls applying the "Symptom Tests."
    *   [ ] **STOP:** When a client verbally agrees to a project, execute your "Just-In-Time" expenses:
        *   [ ] Purchase Tech E&O and General Liability Insurance (~$60/mo).
        *   [ ] Purchase or commission the drafting of your Master Services Agreement (MSA) template ($50 - $800).
    *   [ ] Send the MSA and Statement of Work (SOW) for digital signature.
    *   [ ] Commence technical development.

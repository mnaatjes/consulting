# General Rules to Remember & Trueisms

1. In consulting, you aren't selling a finished app; you are selling your ability to solve a specific technical problem
2. Clients don't need to know the project was abandoned because you got bored; they need to know you proved a technical concept
3. Limit to Local Businesses in Kalamazoo, MI and Portage, MI area (131 Corredore)
4. B2B consulting is entirely based on trust. It is exponentially easier to close a $2,000 deal over a coffee in Kalamazoo than via a cold email to a startup in San Francisco.


# Follow-up Questions

1. What is the legal exposure for Consulting? What kind of protections do I need (e.g. LLC)?
2. What kind of Contracts do I need (term-length, deliverables, etc)?
3. What are my "Products" on offer?
4. Once we identify products, we will need pitch scripts.
5. How can we automate the discovery flow of clients (bots, scrapers, etc)?
6. It seems each of the major products have a discovery phase - how will that be integrated into fee structure?
  - Should there be an introductory pitch for $500 for discovery phase?
  - How can I guard myself against walking into a massive project beyond the scope of the $1500 fee or my timeline?
7. How can I track metrics of success from cold-calling businesses to landing clients to customer satisfaction?
8. How can I build budgeting tracking? Already have startup costs like LLC and Business Insurance.
